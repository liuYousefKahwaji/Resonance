# Resonance v3.1.0

This update makes it easier to discover music, keep your listening moving, and stay connected to YouTube across Windows and Android.

## Discover more music

- Added a personalized YouTube Music Home with Quick Picks, Suggestions, Speed Dial, New Releases, Forgotten Favorites, and more.
- Browse artwork-rich shelves designed to feel at home on both Windows and Android.
- Play a playlist or collection as a full queue, or use its stream and download actions when you want the whole set.

## A better listening flow

- Search and Home results now continue naturally through the list, loop when they reach the end, and stay available from the full-screen player.
- Windows shelves can be moved by grabbing and dragging, with smoother touch and mouse interactions.
- Streaming and downloads recover more reliably when YouTube temporarily changes or rejects a playable source.

## Easier YouTube access

- Connect a signed-in browser session on Windows or import a cookies.txt session on Android.
- The setup guides make it clearer how to connect YouTube Music and replace an expired session.
- Existing libraries, playlists, downloads, and settings remain intact.

Thanks for using Resonance!
