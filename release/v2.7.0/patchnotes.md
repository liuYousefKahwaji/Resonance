(features and fixes across issues #50–#56)

## Themes:
- Obsidian now has a full purple-styled look when full styling is enabled, while disabled styling keeps the classic Resonance appearance
- added Quartz, a white and silver theme
- added Aurum, a warm gold theme

## Playback and Standalone Player:
- long song titles now scroll smoothly without being cut off
- the standalone player now has a more expressive animated gradient background
- tapping the standalone album cover reveals a spinning vinyl record from underneath it; tap again to tuck it away
- metadata edits now save reliably for every track, including the final songs in a list

## Equalizer:
- Custom equalizer curves are remembered when switching between presets
- Global and per-track playback settings keep their own Custom curves
- Custom equalizer settings remain available after restarting or using the companion controls

## Android Widget:
- the playback widget keeps its default compact layout while supporting a controlled expanded size
