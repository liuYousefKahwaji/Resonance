# v3.6.1

## Play just the part you want

- Trim a local song's playback without changing its audio file. The saved section applies wherever that song appears in your library.
- Choose the section with draggable waveform edges, zoom in for finer adjustments, or enter exact times. Preview it before saving, and use the current playback position to set either edge.
- Trimmed songs have a small scissors shortcut. The seekbar keeps the full song's timestamps, with the excluded parts dimmed and seeking limited to your section.
- Reset a song to full playback anytime. Saved sections are included in complete library backups.
