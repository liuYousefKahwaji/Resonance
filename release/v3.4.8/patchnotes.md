# Resonance 3.4.8

## Favorites

- Add or remove favorites from a track's menu or the selection toolbar. You can also favorite or unfavorite an entire playlist at once.
- Gold stars and shuffle icons make favorites easy to recognize across all playlists, with a gold playing indicator for the current favorite.
- Enable **Favorites first** in Sort tracks to keep favorites at the top while preserving your chosen order within each group.
- A permanent **Favorites** playlist brings all your favorite tracks together.

## Discover

- Added **Playlist Library** above Quick Picks in YouTube Music. Browse and play the playlists saved to your connected account, or add their tracks to Resonance.
