# v3.7.0

- Shuffle now creates a fresh queue each time it reaches the end, without immediately repeating the last song.
- Press Shuffle again to try Random: choosing a track or moving to the next or previous song reshuffles the queue. Press once more to return to normal playback.
- Added **Song/Video (beta)** to the expanded YouTube player. Switch between audio and available music videos in place of the album artwork. Video playback is still being refined.
- Collapsing the player or putting the app in the background returns to audio-only playback.
