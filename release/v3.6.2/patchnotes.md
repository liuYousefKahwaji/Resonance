# v3.6.2

- **Voice chat on Windows:** Share music and your microphone together in Discord, games, and other voice apps. Open the microphone button beside the volume control to adjust music and microphone levels separately, choose devices, or hear your own microphone. Requires a one-time VB-CABLE setup. If Discord filters or chops up the music, try its **Studio** input profile.
- **Launch on startup:** A new Windows setting opens Resonance when you sign in. The window appears normally, even with tray mode enabled.
- **Choose your search source:** Switch between **YouTube Music** for songs and **YouTube** for regular videos using the tabs below search. Your choice is remembered, and switching reruns the current query.
- **Track dates:** Search results and artist pages now show release or upload dates where available. Older tracks show full dates when provided; recent uploads use labels such as **Today**, **Yesterday**, or **2 hours ago**. Dates load separately so results and playback don't have to wait.
