# Resonance 3.6.0

- **Offline playlists:** Download a playlist's streamed songs for offline listening, with progress, individual failures and retry options. Existing downloads are reused, and removing offline downloads keeps imported files and songs needed by other playlists or Favorites.
- **Library browsing:** Browse local albums by cover and open their tracklists. On Windows, add an entire folder, including its subfolders, without adding duplicate files.
- **Smart playlists:** Create playlists that update automatically using rules such as artist, favorites, offline availability, date added and listening history. Start with presets, combine rules, choose sorting and limits, or save the results as a regular playlist.
- **Resonance Wrapped:** View yearly, monthly and all-time listening recaps, including top songs, artists and playlists, listening streaks and local versus streamed playback. Save a themed recap card as an image. Statistics are stored locally and begin with newly recorded listening; tracking can be disabled or cleared.
- **Backup and restore:** Export settings alone or a complete backup with playlists, Favorites, track settings, history and statistics. Audio files are optional. Preview backups before restoring, merge or replace your data, and relink moved files. Browser connections and paired devices must be reconnected separately.
- **More reliable updates:** Automatic checks now recover after skipped or failed checks and continue during longer sessions. Known updates remain available in Settings, and cancelled downloads can be retried without restarting.
- **Search polish:** The search arrow becomes an animated clear button when text is entered, making it easier to start a new search.
- **YouTube Music playlist fix:** Fixed some songs incorrectly appearing unavailable when played from a playlist despite working from their author page.
