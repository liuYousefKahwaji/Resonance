# Smoother search and small quality-of-life fixes
(solves #45)

## Search:
- results now appear automatically after you pause typing, with a quick two-song preview
- pressing Enter loads the full set of results
- the keyboard closes when you tap elsewhere on the search screen

## Song identification:
- tapping outside the song-identification box returns you to the music list

## Suggested Music:
- suggestions recover correctly if a search is started while they are loading

## Compatibility:
- existing playlists, downloads, and saved settings continue to work
