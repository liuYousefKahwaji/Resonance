# Resonance 4.0.0 — The Overhaul

Resonance 4.0 redesigns the listening experience, from browsing your library to controlling playback, recovering failed streams and continuing where you left off.

## A redesigned Resonance

- **The new listening layout is now the default.** Discover, Search and Library have dedicated navigation, with playlist shortcuts on Windows and compact navigation on Android. Prefer the familiar look? Turn off **New listening layout** in Settings.
- **A refreshed library and Discover.** Larger artwork, cleaner track lists, playlist headers and a cover grid make browsing more visual and easier to scan.
- **A redesigned player.** The compact player brings track details, transport, seeking, volume and queue controls together. Android gains easier access to shuffle/random, repeat and Playback Settings, with more room for controls in the full player.
- **Windows prompts that fit the desktop**, with clearer spacing, compact buttons and separate action areas in both layouts.
- **A new optional cinematic opening**, with a light-and-title sequence built around the original Resonance sound. Tap to skip; reduced-motion settings are supported.

## Faster playback, better recovery

- **Switch songs without waiting.** Selecting another stream immediately takes over from a pending request; late responses cannot bring back the previous selection.
- **Recover without getting stuck.** Failed streams offer Retry and, for YouTube tracks, Find another upload. Choose a replacement yourself while keeping your saved playlist unchanged.
- **Less waiting on downloads.** Original-format audio is now the default, avoiding unnecessary conversion. MP3 remains available, and recently prepared audio links can be reused.
- **Smoother Song/Video switching**, with reduced preparation delays and better audio/video alignment. Video mode remains in beta.

## More forgiving everyday use

- **Pick up where you left off.** Your queue, shuffle order and playback position return when you reopen Resonance, paused until you press Play.
- **Undo playlist edits.** Quickly reverse track removals, playlist deletion, sorting, reordering, bulk copy/move and bulk Favorites changes. Deleted playlists return as local copies; permanent audio-file deletion is excluded.
- **Track links within reach.** Copy a source link or open it in YouTube or YouTube Music from track menus, including downloads with a saved YouTube source.
- **Playback Settings is easier to find**, through the player controls and Settings. Also fixed a Windows crash when opening Settings.
