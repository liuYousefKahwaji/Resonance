# Resonance 3.4.6

- Sort playlists by name, date added, or a saved random order, with ascending and descending options. Find **Sort tracks** in the playlist menu. Playback shuffle remains separate.
- Save individual song volume adjustments from −100% to +100% in Playback Settings. Positive boosts pause while the main volume booster is active.
- See update download sizes and savings when a smaller update is available. On Android, follow update progress and continue installation from Settings.
