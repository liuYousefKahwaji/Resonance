# Resonance 4.1.0

## Equalizer

- Reworked the equalizer on Windows and Android so bass, mids, and treble adjustments change the sound of both local music and streams. You can hear the change as you move a slider.
- Boosting a band keeps more of the track’s volume at normal listening levels. The equalizer shows when the current audio output can’t apply the effect.

## Lyrics

- Expanded word-synced lyrics with QQ Music and support for LRCLIB’s word timestamps. Resonance checks several sources together and uses word timing when available, with line-synced and plain lyrics as fallbacks.
- Fixed missing spaces in Better Lyrics and word highlighting in LRCLIB. Search results and the player show the timing each version actually provides.
- Choose Lyrics now offers results from multiple sources. Filter by source, timing, or how close the duration is to your track, with options for matches within 3, 10, or 30 seconds.

## Play Next

- A Play Next icon fades in beside tracks in Library, Discover, or Search when that page’s queue is active. Tap it to put a track immediately after the one you’re playing.
- Each playlist or listening session keeps its own Play Next requests. After they play, the original queue continues with your current playback mode, including Shuffle and Random. Browsing another playlist keeps your current queue in place.
