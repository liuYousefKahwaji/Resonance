# v4.0.1

- Improved lyric searches and cleaned up YouTube track names. Server outages now show clearer messages and a countdown before retrying.
- Added a faint pulse to the active track highlight.
- Playlist shortcuts now show their cover artwork. The sidebar can smoothly collapse to icons, and remembers your preference.
- Fixed Android queue controls and enabled choosing upcoming tracks from the queue.
- Added smooth Android swipes between Library and Search, and from Search to Discover. Pages follow your finger and keep their state when you return; Discover shelves keep their horizontal scrolling.
- Improved Android Google Drive account restoration to avoid a sign-in prompt on every launch. Existing connections may need one reconnect after updating.
- Made the optional track-list motion blur more visible during fast scrolling, with a quick fade when scrolling stops.
