# Resonance v3.4.0

This release brings music discovery into the main player, speeds up YouTube browsing and playback, and makes stream switching more dependable.

## Discover and library

- Switch between **Discover** and your local library from the main window. Choose which one opens first in **Settings → Listening focus**; Resonance remembers your choice.
- Browse YouTube Music Home, search YouTube, play streams, and add songs to playlists without leaving Discover. The download queue remains available there, too.
- On Android, tap **Discover** or **Resonance** in the header to switch views. Discover's bottom player starts collapsed, opens when you first play a song, and can be collapsed or expanded again at any time.
- YouTube Music Home has a simpler layout. Pull down to refresh it on Android, or use the small refresh button on Windows.
- Discovery tabs and song actions have a cleaner, less crowded layout.

## Faster YouTube listening

- YouTube search responds sooner while you type, with faster result loading on Android and Windows.
- YouTube Music Home shows its first shelves sooner, fills in more as you browse, and can show previously loaded shelves immediately when you return.
- Opening a stream brings up the player while the audio connects. Resonance prepares likely songs and nearby queue tracks in the background, so many starts and skips need less waiting.
- Stream playback and next/previous controls are more reliable, including rapid song changes. The player now keeps the selected song and the audio you hear in sync and recovers more promptly from a stalled stream.
- Suggested Music and YouTube listening history load more promptly.

## Lyrics

- Timed Arabic and Hebrew lyrics display and highlight in the correct reading direction, including lines that begin with punctuation.
