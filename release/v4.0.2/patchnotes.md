# Resonance 4.0.2

- A cleaner Android mini-player and navigation bar in the new listening layout, with matching colors, scrolling song titles, Playback Settings and Play/Pause. Compact controls are on by default; turn them off in Settings for more controls.
- Open the full player with a tap or upward swipe, and return with a smooth downward transition. Song/Video labels now turn into icons after a few seconds.
- Refined the player bar colors on Windows and Android.
- Downloads can be canceled, including the current track in a playlist import. The download queue now opens from every Android tab.
- Failed Drive downloads retry up to three times, then wait for a manual Retry. Clearing the queue stops them coming back; tracks remain available as streams.
- More consistent artwork after editing covers, plus better recovery of missing artwork on downloaded tracks.
- Smoother startup animation and restored seek-step controls in the expanded player controls.
