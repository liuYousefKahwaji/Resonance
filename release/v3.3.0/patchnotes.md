# Resonance v3.3.0

This release makes recent listening easier to revisit and improves streamed artwork throughout the app.

## Listening history

- Added a dedicated History page in the main header with **YouTube Music** and **Resonance** tabs.
- YouTube Music history loads its tracks first, then fills in views and likes in the background.
- Play a YouTube history track immediately, add it as a stream to the active playlist, or download it.
- Resonance remembers up to 100 distinct local tracks after three seconds of real playback.
- Replaying a local track moves it to the top; streams, pauses, and seek jumps do not create local history entries.
- Removing or clearing listening history never deletes audio files.

## Better stream artwork

- Streamed tracks show their existing thumbnail immediately for fast loading.
- A higher-resolution YouTube artwork variant loads in the background and crossfades in when ready.
- The progressive artwork treatment now appears in the library, compact player, full-screen player, queue, and History.
- If a higher-resolution image is unavailable, the original thumbnail remains visible without flashing or disappearing.

## Included improvements

- Search active playlists by title or artist.
- Download streamed playlist entries in place from their three-dot menu.
- Select Windows playback output devices.
- Use authenticated YouTube Music Home, session queues, playback recovery, and optional YouTube Music history reporting.
