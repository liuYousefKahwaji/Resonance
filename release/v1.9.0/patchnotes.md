# Another **Big** Update:
(#7, #8, and #9. All changes are for both platforms)

## Theme Styles:
Added new theme styles, can be changed from settings
* **$\color{#9827F5}{\textsf{\textbf{\Large Obsidian}}}$**: The default violet/purple style
* **$\color{#1DB954}{\textsf{\textbf{\Large Jade}}}$**: Green, similar to spotify
* **$\color{#0047AB}{\textsf{\textbf{\Large Cobalt}}}$**: Blue
* **$\color{#FF0000}{\textsf{\textbf{\Large Magma}}}$**: Red, similar to youtube music
* **$\color{#444444}{\textsf{\textbf{\Large Void}}}$**: black, oled feel

## Completely Reworked Youtube System:
* replaced the "youtube" button with a search button
* searching now produces 10 results instead of 5
* searching now takes up a full screen, displays all information about the results including thumbnails, and never overflows
* there are 3 buttons for each search; stream, download, and play. 
* Stream and download lead back to the playlist after finishing, normal
* Play allows a standalone playlist-free player for the chosen track, streamed, doesnt get added to the playlist. 
* going to and from the standalone player has a smooth transition

## Other Noteworthy Changes:
* the currently playing click leads back to the standalone player when necessary
* fixed album resolution
* added a redirect and highlight after a track is downloaded/streamed into a playlist
