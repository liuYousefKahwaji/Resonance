# Resonance 4.0.3

- Improved cover editing with a larger preview, crop and zoom controls, and separate options to remove a cover or restore the original. Fixed chosen covers reverting to the previous image.
- Swipe the compact Android player left for the next track or right for the previous one. Swipe up still opens the full player.
- The download queue now shows file size, download speed and estimated time remaining when available, with clearer messages for connection, storage and audio-processing failures.
