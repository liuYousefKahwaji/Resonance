# v3.8.0

- Connect Google Drive to save playlists and bring them to your other devices.
- Enable **Keep in sync** to share playlist names, track changes and order automatically. Choose whether new tracks stream or download on each device; offline edits catch up when you reconnect.
- Browse music folders in Drive, play or download their tracks, and upload local music from files or playlists.
- Save copies of Drive playlists, replace them, or move them to Trash without deleting local music.
- Faster playlist transfers, quicker track names, and improved download startup and reliability on Windows and Android.
- New synced tracks are available to stream immediately while their downloads finish. Failed downloads leave the streaming version available to play and retry.

On Android, sync checks while Resonance is running and catches up when you reopen it. Removing a track from a synced playlist keeps its downloaded audio file.
